Our Story

Crafting Comfort
Since 2018

More Than Just
Furniture Makers

It started in a small workshop in Alok Nagar Mushakedi, Indore with a simple vision: to bring new life to old furniture. Today, AK Sofa Cushion stands as a symbol of trust and quality craftsmanship.

We realized that people don't want to throw away their favorite sofas just because the foam has sagged or the fabric is torn. They want to preserve the memories attached to them. That's where we step in.

"Our mission is not just to repair, but to upgrade. When we return your furniture, it should look and feel better than the day you bought it."
