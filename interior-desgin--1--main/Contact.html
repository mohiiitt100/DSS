Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide home visits?

Yes, we provide free home visits in Indore. We bring fabric catalogs and foam samples so you can choose in the comfort of your home.

Absolutely. We specialize in refurbishing old sofas. We change the fabric, replace the foam, and polish the wood to make it look brand new.

Yes, we make custom mattresses (Gadde) of any size and thickness using high-density foam, bonded foam, or cotton as per your requirement.
